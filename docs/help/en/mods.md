# Mods

The Mods tab of an instance lists every mod in it. You can search, turn mods off without deleting them, and update them.

## How to
- Open an instance and choose the **Mods** tab (Alt+2).
- Type in the search box, or use the chips: **All**, **Off**, **Updates**, **Problems**.
- Click a mod to see its details on the right.
- Tick several mods to get the bulk bar: turn off, turn on or delete them together. **Undo** is there right after.
- **Add mods › Find online…** opens Discover showing only mods that fit this instance. You can also drag .jar files onto the window.
- After Jay finds updates, **Update all** appears.

## If something goes wrong
- A mod you deleted is in [Recently deleted](help:recycle) for a few days.
- The game crashes after adding mods: see [Help me fix…](help:fix-helper).
