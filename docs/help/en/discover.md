# Discover: mods, modpacks, resource packs, shaders

Search Modrinth and CurseForge and install content into your instances.

## How to
- Pick a type with the chips at the top and a source with the segmented control.
- Click **+ Queue** on a result (or press Space) to put it in the queue bar, then click **Install N** to install them all at once; required mods come along.
- Click a result for details: a side column on wide windows, a drawer on narrow ones. Esc closes it.

## If something goes wrong
- CurseForge finds nothing: it needs your own CurseForge key in [Settings › Downloads and services](jay:settings#CurseForgeKey).
- Jay contacts Modrinth and CurseForge only while you use this page or click "Check for updates", never in the background.
