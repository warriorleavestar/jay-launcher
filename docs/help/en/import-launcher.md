# Coming from another launcher

Jay can play the instances you already have in another launcher's game folder, without moving or copying them.

## How to
- In the library choose **New instance**, then **From another launcher**. Jay lists the game folders it found.
- Choose **Use this folder**, or **Choose a folder…** if yours isn't listed.
- Jay plays the instances where they are. Separate folders keep their own settings.
- The first-start guide also lists the games it finds; **Add to library** does the same.

## If something goes wrong
- Nothing found: choose the folder yourself (the one with "versions" in it).
