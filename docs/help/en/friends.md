# Friends

Friends are people you have checked once. After that you invite each other without sending codes through a chat app.

## How to
- Add: send your **friend code** to a friend, or paste theirs under **Add a friend…**. When they accept, the two of you compare four emoji, by voice or in person. If they match, you are friends.
- See a friend: click them on the Friends page. You see their Jay account, each of their devices and its safety code.
- Invite: open a room first, then use **Invite to my room** on the Friends page, or **Invite a friend** in the room.
- Remove a friend or one of their devices: **Manage friends**.

## Good to know
- A friend code only lets people ask to add you. It is not an invite, and your address is not in it.
- Invites to friends travel sealed through a public network. The line at the bottom of the page says which one, or that none is connected. The network status at the top right shows the same on every page.
- With a Jay account on both sides, a friend's new computer is added by itself and you get a notice. Without one, each computer is added separately.
- With "For a child" on, public networks are off, so this page is empty.
