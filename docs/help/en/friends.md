# Friends

Friends are people you have checked once. After that you invite each other without sending codes through a chat app. People are on the left, the conversation with the one you picked on the right.

## How to
- Add a friend: click **Add a friend**. Send them your **friend code**, or paste theirs. When they accept, the two of you compare four emoji, by voice or in person. If they match, click **They match, add as a friend**. This is the only way to add someone.
- See a friend: click them. Under the name is what Jay knows right now: checked, in the room with you, in which room of theirs, or a new device to confirm.
- Safety codes and devices: click **End-to-end encrypted · checked** above the conversation. There you can remove one of their devices, or **Remove from friends**.
- Invite: open a room first, then click **Invite to my room**. The conversation gets a line that says Sent, Delivered or Not delivered yet.
- Join their room: once they invite you, **Join “room name”** shows here.
- Message: for now, only while you are both in the same room. Otherwise the box is greyed out and the reason is under it.
- **Let them straight in when they knock**: with it on, they come in with any invite of yours that still works, without asking you first. This switch is the old "trusted people" list.
- Back up and restore the friend list: **⋯** at the top right. The backup is locked with your Jay account.

## Good to know
- **People I've met** came into your room but never compared emoji with you. You can **Add as a friend**, or only turn on "Let them straight in". After the room closes, only people with the switch on stay listed.
- When a friend has a new device, you confirm it with **It's them**. Until then that device still asks when it knocks.
- A friend code only lets people ask to add you. It is not an invite, and your address is not in it.
- Invites to friends travel sealed through a public network. The line at the top says which one. If none is connected, click **Connect** next to it.
- A conversation lives only while Jay runs. Nothing of it is written to disk.
- With a Jay account on both sides, a friend's new computer is added by itself and you get a notice. Without one, each computer is added separately.
- With "For a child" on, public networks are off, so this page shows only a note, and every knock asks you.
