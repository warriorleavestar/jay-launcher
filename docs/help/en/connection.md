# Connection problems and relays

If you can't connect to a friend, one check tells you why and what to try.

![The Connection problems? drawer](shot:connection)

## How to
- Choose **Connection problems?** at the bottom of Multiplayer, or press Ctrl+Shift+D.
- **Check my network** takes a few seconds and gives one plain sentence first. The technical details are folded away.
- Try what it suggests, for example **Try another relay**.
- Relays are in **Settings › Network › Relays**. Invites need one; a direct connection doesn't. Using the same relays as your friend makes connecting easier.

## If something goes wrong
- Still stuck: export the diagnostics from the same drawer and send them to someone who can help.
