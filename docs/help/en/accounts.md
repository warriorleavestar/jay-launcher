# Accounts: Microsoft, offline, skin sites

Your account decides your name and skin in the game, and whether you can join online-mode servers.

## How to
- Microsoft account: click **Sign in with Microsoft** and sign in in your browser.
- Offline account: just a name; good for single player and LAN.
- Skin-site account: enter the skin site's address, your account and password.
- Switch: click your avatar at the bottom left to open **Me**, then click **Use** on an account.
- The skin figure: drag the figure on the "Me" page to turn it, or click it and use ← and →. Offline accounts get a figure drawn from their name.

## What else is on Me
- **Jay account**: one identity for multiplayer on every computer you use, made from 12 words you write down.
- **My friend code**: send it to a friend so they can ask to add you. It lets nobody into your rooms and says nothing about where you are.
- **My devices**: this computer and its safety code. Without a Jay account, friends have to add you again after you change computers.
- **Appearance**: light or dark, the language, and the way to theme packs.

## If something goes wrong
- Microsoft sign-in fails: check that your computer's clock is right; a child Microsoft account needs a parent to allow multiplayer in Xbox family settings.
- Sign-ins are stored only on your computer, encrypted.
