# Accounts: Microsoft, offline, skin sites

Your account decides your name and skin in the game, and whether you can join online-mode servers.

## How to
- Microsoft account: click **Sign in with Microsoft** and sign in in your browser.
- Offline account: just a name; good for single player and LAN.
- Skin-site account: enter the skin site's address, your account and password.
- Switch: click your avatar at the bottom left and pick an account.

## If something goes wrong
- Microsoft sign-in fails: check that your computer's clock is right; a child Microsoft account needs a parent to allow multiplayer in Xbox family settings.
- Sign-ins are stored only on your computer, encrypted.
