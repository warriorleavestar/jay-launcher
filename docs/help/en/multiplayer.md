# Multiplayer

Play with friends: open a room and send your friend an invite code; your friend pastes it and knocks, and comes in once you say yes.

![The Play together tab with the Open a room and Join a friend cards](shot:multiplayer-lobby)

## How to
- Open a room: on the Multiplayer page click **Open a room**, pick the instance, then click **Invite friends** (Ctrl+Shift+I).
- Join: paste the invite code you were given and click **Knock**, then wait for a yes.
- Someone knocks: a card appears in your room; click **Allow** or **Decline** (Alt+Y / Alt+N).
- Safety code: if both of you see the same safety code, nobody is pretending to be someone else in between.

## If something goes wrong
- Cannot connect: run "Check network"; it tells you which step fails.
- For a child: remote control, public networks and more can be turned off; see [For a child](help:child-mode).
