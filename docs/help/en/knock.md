# Knocks and safety codes

When someone joins your room with an invite, they knock first. You decide who comes in.

![Drill mode: practise answering a knock](shot:drill)

## How to
- A knock shows a locked card with the person's name and safety code. The focus starts on **Deny**, so Enter never lets anyone in by accident.
- Ask your friend to read out the safety code on their screen. Let them in only if it is exactly the same.
- Alt+Y lets them in, Alt+N denies. Knocks also show in the request bar and a small window on top, and answering one answers all.
- **Room settings** set how long a knock waits: 2 minutes, 10 minutes or until you answer.

## If something goes wrong
- One character is different: deny and ask your friend to knock again. Someone may be in between.
- Practise it: the lobby's **Practice once ›** walks through a knock with a wrong code.
