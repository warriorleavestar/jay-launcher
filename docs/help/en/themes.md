# Light, dark and theme packs

Jay can follow your system, or always be light or dark. Theme packs change colours, fonts and corners.

![Settings › Appearance](shot:appearance)

## How to
- **Settings › Appearance › Theme**: System, Light or Dark.
- Theme packs: import a .jaytheme file, or drag it onto the window. It applies at once.
- Built in: **Jay classic amber** and **High contrast**. They can't be deleted.
- An older theme pack is upgraded when Jay reads it; its card says so, and **Copy for the author** copies what changed.
- Theme packs can't change safety notices or the privacy text.

## If something goes wrong
- A theme pack stopped Jay from opening: Jay starts with the default look next time by itself. You can also start Jay with --default-theme.
