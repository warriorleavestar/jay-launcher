# Resource packs and shaders

Resource packs change textures and sounds; shaders change the lighting. Jay keeps the same pack list as the game's own Resource Packs screen.

## How to
- Open an instance and choose **Resource packs**. Packs are in two lists, **On** and **Off**. Packs higher in the list win.
- Space turns the selected pack on or off; Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ move it.
- A tag says when a pack was made for an older or newer version. It may still work; some textures can look wrong.
- Shaders need the Iris mod. Without it, the **Shaders** tab shows a banner with **Install Iris**.
- Pick a shader to use it, or choose **No shaders**.

## If something goes wrong
- The game crashes with shaders on: the "last session" panel can turn shaders off for the next start. Your shader packs stay.
- A deleted pack is in [Recently deleted](help:recycle).
