# Sending files and remote help

You can send a friend or someone in your room a file, a world, a mod list, a screenshot, a log or a folder. They accept each one first. You can also ask to control their computer to help them, which also needs their yes.

## Sending a file
- In a friend's conversation, click the paperclip left of the text box and choose what to send, or drag files onto the conversation.
- In a room, click a person, then **Send a file**. In an instance's Worlds tab, each world has **Send to a friend**.
- Worlds and folders are zipped first; the world in your game stays as it is. A mod list sends names and versions, not the mods. A log is masked before it goes (your computer's user name, tokens, email addresses).
- A card in the conversation shows the progress, direct or relayed, and the speed. You can **Cancel**.
- When a friend isn't in the same room, the file waits on this computer and starts by itself when you are in the same room. A send that broke part way carries on from where it stopped next time.

## Receiving a file
- When someone sends you a file, the request bar at the top says who it is, whether they are a checked friend, and what kind of file it is. Decline is the default; Alt+Y accepts, Alt+N declines. No answer within five minutes counts as not accepted.
- Received files are only saved to Downloads/Jay (you can change this in [Settings › Multiplayer › Files](jay:settings#FilesFolder)). Jay never opens them by itself, and warns you when one is a program.
- A received world has **Import into an instance** on its card. A received mod list has **Compare mods**, which says how many mods are missing or different.

## Remote help
- Click a person in the room and ask to see their screen under "Share with them". They must accept every time. While it runs their screen has a red border, and Ctrl+Shift+F12 stops it at once.

## Good to know
- Files are end-to-end encrypted: the host and relays can't see what's in them.
- Settings › Multiplayer › Files can accept files from checked friends without asking (off by default; programs always ask) and limit the speed.
- Remote help is only for people you trust. Nobody from Jay will ever ask you for it. For a child, files come only from friends and remote help can be turned off: see [For a child](help:child-mode).
