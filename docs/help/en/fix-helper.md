# Help me fix…

When a crash comes from one of many mods, **Help me fix…** finds it by halving: it turns half the mods off, you try the game, and it narrows it down.

## How to
- On the "last session" panel, choose **Still not working? › Help me fix…**.
- Jay turns some mods off for now. Start the game, play a little, then choose **Still crashes** or **Works now**.
- The card says which round it is and about how many are left. You can switch instances or restart Jay; it carries on.
- When Jay finds the mod, **Turn off** turns off only that one. Every other mod comes back on.

## If something goes wrong
- **Restore everything** puts every mod back as it was, at any time.
- Fewer than two mods on: there is nothing to halve. Look at the [log](help:logs) instead.
