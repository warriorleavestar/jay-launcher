# Words in Jay

Short, plain meanings of the words Jay uses.

## Words
- **Instance**: one game setup with its own version, mods and worlds.
- **Loader**: what lets mods run, such as Fabric, Forge, NeoForge or Quilt.
- **Room**: your world plus a private way in for the friends you invite.
- **Invite code**: what you send a friend so they can knock on your room.
- **Safety code**: a code both sides see. If they are the same, nobody is in between.
- **Friend code**: finds a friend's room in public rooms.
- **Relay server**: a server that helps two computers reach each other when they can't directly.
- **Connected directly / via relay**: whether your games talk straight to each other or through a relay server.
